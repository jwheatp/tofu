---
description: Interview technique for the challenger - one question at a time until nothing is open.
user-invocable: false
---
- Ask one question at a time, with your recommended answer, in plain language.
- Prefer concrete questions ("What happens after 5 failed attempts?") to abstract ones.
- Record each unanswered question under Open questions; each answer becomes a rule change plus one Decisions line.
- Stop when Open questions is empty; do not invent questions to look thorough.
