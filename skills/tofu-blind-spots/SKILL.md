---
description: Checklist of blind spots to hunt in a spec.
user-invocable: false
---
Check each rule and screen against: empty/loading/error/success states; insufficient rights; invalid, huge or missing input; concurrency and repeat actions; time (expiry, time zones); data already existing (migration, defaults); failure of external services; accessibility and mobile; privacy (personal data, logs); undo and deletion; limits and quotas; notification and audit trail.
Raise only the ones that apply, as questions.
